Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

MyContext is a Mac app that downloads your Gmail to your own computer, removes sensitive information from it on your computer, and lets AI assistants you choose search the result. This policy explains what data MyContext uses and where it goes.

The short version

  • Your email is processed and stored only on your Mac. MyContext has no servers, and the developer never receives, stores or can access your email.
  • Sensitive data is removed before anything is saved.
  • Nothing is shared unless you ask an AI assistant a question, and then only the scrubbed results relevant to that question are sent.
  • MyContext only reads your mail. It cannot send, delete or change email.

Google data MyContext accesses

With your permission, MyContext uses one of these read-only Gmail permissions, depending on the mode you choose:

  • gmail.readonly (full mode): to read your messages and their labels so they can be scrubbed and searched on your Mac.
  • gmail.metadata (headers-only mode): to read message headers such as sender, recipients, subject and date, without message bodies.

MyContext never downloads the contents of attachments. It records only each attachment's file name, type and size.

How your data is processed and stored

  • Scrubbing happens on your Mac, in memory, before storage. Whole messages are skipped if they are spam or trash, promotions, social or forum notifications, bulk mail, security messages (such as verification codes and password resets), or come from banking, tax or health senders. In the messages that remain, values such as one-time codes, passwords, card and bank numbers, Social Security numbers, API keys, dates of birth, phone numbers and street addresses are replaced with placeholders. Quoted replies and signatures are removed.
  • Only scrubbed messages are saved, in a database on your Mac (in ~/Library/Application Support/MyContext) that only your user account can read.
  • Your Google sign-in token is stored in your Mac's Keychain.
  • MyContext has no analytics, tracking or telemetry.

When data leaves your Mac

Only in these cases, each started by you:

  • Asking Grok. When you ask a question in MyContext, your question and the scrubbed messages that Grok's searches return are sent to xAI, using an xAI API key that you provide. xAI handles that data under its own terms and privacy policy, which you agree to with xAI directly.
  • Other AI apps you connect. If you connect another AI app to MyContext (through the Model Context Protocol), that app can search your scrubbed messages, under that app's own terms.
  • Opening a message in Gmail opens it in your web browser at Google.

The developer does not sell your data, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

MyContext's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your scrubbed mail stays on your Mac until you delete it. In MyContext, Settings → Disconnect and delete local mail removes it and revokes MyContext's access to your Google account.
  • You can also revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling MyContext and deleting its folder removes everything it stored.

Children

MyContext is not intended for children under 13.

Changes
