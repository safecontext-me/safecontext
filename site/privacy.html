SafeContext Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

SafeContext is a Mac app, developed by [[DEVELOPER NAME]] (“we”, “the developer”), that downloads your Gmail to your own computer, removes sensitive information from it on your computer, and lets you and AI assistants you choose search the result. This policy explains what data SafeContext uses, how it is protected, and where it goes. It applies to the SafeContext app and to this website, safecontext.me.

The short version

  • Your email is processed and stored only on your Mac. SafeContext has no servers, and the developer never receives, stores or can access your email.
  • Sensitive data is removed before anything is saved.
  • Nothing leaves your Mac unless you ask an AI assistant a question or connect an AI app, and then only scrubbed messages are shared.
  • SafeContext only reads your mail. It cannot send, delete or change email.
  • Your Google data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI models.

Google data SafeContext accesses

With your permission, SafeContext uses one of these read-only Gmail permissions, depending on the mode you choose when you connect:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly (full mode): to read your messages and their labels so they can be scrubbed and searched on your Mac.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.metadata (headers-only mode): to read message headers such as sender, recipients, subject and date, without message bodies.

SafeContext also learns the email address of the Google account you connect, so it can show which account is connected. It never downloads the contents of attachments; it records only each attachment's file name, type and size.

How SafeContext uses your Google data

SafeContext uses Gmail data only to provide the features you see in the app:

  • building a scrubbed, searchable copy of your mail on your Mac;
  • letting you browse and search that copy;
  • answering questions you ask about your mail, with links back to the original messages in Gmail; and
  • letting AI apps that you connect search that copy on your behalf.

How your data is processed and stored

  • Scrubbing happens on your Mac, in memory, before storage. Whole messages are skipped if they are spam or trash, promotions, social or forum notifications, bulk mail, security messages (such as verification codes and password resets), or come from banking, tax or health senders. In the messages that remain, values such as one-time codes, passwords, card and bank numbers, Social Security numbers, API keys, dates of birth, phone numbers and street addresses are replaced with placeholders. Quoted replies and signatures are removed.
  • Only scrubbed messages are saved, in a database file on your Mac, in ~/Library/Application Support/SafeContext.
  • Your Google sign-in token and, if you add one, your xAI API key are stored in your Mac's Keychain, not in a plain file.
  • SafeContext has no analytics, tracking or telemetry, and this website sets no cookies.

How your data is protected

We want to be precise about this:

  • The SafeContext folder and database are created so that only your macOS user account can read or write them.
  • The Google sign-in token is protected by the macOS Keychain.
  • The database itself is not separately encrypted by SafeContext. It is protected by your Mac's own security: your user account password and, if you turn it on, FileVault disk encryption. We recommend keeping FileVault on.
  • Because sensitive values are scrubbed before saving, the stored copy holds less sensitive data than your mailbox does.
  • When messages are deleted from the database, SafeContext overwrites the deleted content rather than leaving it in free space in the file.
  • Connections to Google and to xAI use HTTPS.

When data leaves your Mac

Only in the cases below, each started by you. Sharing with an AI assistant is the feature you are asking SafeContext to perform.

  • Asking Grok. When you ask a question in SafeContext, your question and the scrubbed messages that Grok's searches return are sent to xAI, using an xAI API key that you provide. Nothing is sent to xAI until you ask a question. xAI handles that data under its own terms and privacy policy, which you agree to with xAI directly.
  • AI apps you connect. If you connect another AI app to SafeContext (through the Model Context Protocol), that app can search and read your scrubbed messages when you use it. You choose whether to connect an app, and you can disconnect it at any time. The app handles that data under its own terms.
  • Opening a message in Gmail opens it in your web browser at Google.

The developer is never a recipient in any of these cases.

Google API Services User Data Policy and Limited Use

SafeContext's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  1. Limited use. SafeContext uses Google user data only to provide and improve the user-facing features described in this policy, which are prominent in the app.
  2. Limited transfer. SafeContext does not transfer Google user data to anyone except as necessary to provide those features (such as sending scrubbed messages to xAI or to an AI app when you ask it to), with your consent, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets with notice to you.
  3. No human reading. No person, including the developer, reads your Google user data, unless you give explicit consent for specific messages (for example, to help with a support request), it is necessary for security purposes such as investigating abuse, it is required to comply with applicable law, or the data has been aggregated and anonymized for internal operations. Because your data never leaves your Mac except as described above, the developer has no copy of it to read.
  4. No advertising, selling or AI training. SafeContext does not use or transfer Google user data to serve advertisements, including retargeting, personalized or interest-based ads; does not sell it; does not use it to determine credit-worthiness or for lending purposes; and does not use it to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your scrubbed mail stays on your Mac until you delete it. In SafeContext, Settings → Disconnect and delete local mail deletes it and revokes SafeContext's access to your Google account.
  • You can also revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling SafeContext and deleting its folder removes everything it stored.
  • Data you sent to xAI or to an AI app you connected is kept under that service's own policy.
