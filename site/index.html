MyContext

A private context layer for AI. Your email is scrubbed on your Mac before any AI can read it.

  • Stays on your Mac. MyContext downloads your Gmail to your computer. There is no MyContext server.
  • Scrubbed before it's saved. Verification codes, passwords, card and account numbers, API keys, addresses and similar details are removed. Security, banking, tax and health email is skipped entirely.
  • Read-only. MyContext can't send, delete or change your email.
  • You choose what AI sees. When you ask Grok a question, only the scrubbed messages relevant to it are sent.

MyContext is currently in a small private preview.
