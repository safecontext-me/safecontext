What full access really means

Giving an AI agent your whole inbox means it can read everything there, and act on what it reads.

  • It can read tax returns, medical records, bank statements, password resets, and sign-in codes.
  • A single email has already been used to instruct an agent to leak Gmail data (ShadowLeak, 2025, later fixed). A similar flaw in Microsoft 365 Copilot was rated critical and fixed (EchoLeak). OpenAI has said this kind of attack, prompt injection, is unlikely to ever be fully solved.
  • What the AI reads is processed on that company's servers, under that company's terms.

If you're happy giving AI your whole inbox, SafeContext isn't for you. If you've hesitated, it is.

SafeContext does not make prompt injection impossible. It shrinks what any AI can reach: sensitive mail never gets to it, the rest is scrubbed, access is read-only, and your copy stays on your Mac.

What stays private

After your first sync, a Privacy Report shows what was kept, dropped, and redacted. Tax, health, and bank mail never reach any AI. Sensitive personal details are removed too, including ID numbers, Social Security numbers, passwords, and 2FA codes.

Skipped entirely

  • Security mail: sign-in and verification codes, password resets, new sign-in alerts
  • Banking, tax, and health mail, by sender (banks, brokerages, tax services, insurers, pharmacies, clinics) and by subject (statements, tax forms, lab results, prescriptions)
  • Promotions, Social and Forums tabs, newsletters, and other bulk mail
  • Spam, trash, and drafts

Calendar events follow the same security, banking, tax, and health rules. Dial-in PINs and meeting passcodes are never read.

Redacted in what's kept

  • One-time and backup codes
  • Passwords
  • API keys and tokens
  • Private keys
  • Card numbers and CVVs
  • Bank account and routing numbers
  • IBANs
  • ID numbers
  • Social Security numbers
  • Dates of birth
  • Phone numbers
  • Street addresses

Each one becomes a placeholder such as [REDACTED:CREDIT_CARD]. Quoted replies and signatures are trimmed, and attachments are never downloaded, only their file names.

How it works

  1. Connect your sources

    Sign in and grant read-only access. Connect email, calendar, and file storage.

    More coming soon iMessage

  2. Automatically scrubbed on your computer

    Each message and event is cleaned in memory, before anything is saved. Only the redacted, clean version is stored, on your computer. You get a Privacy Report showing what was kept, dropped, and redacted.

  3. Connect your AI

    Turn on MCP, add your private link as a connector in Grok or Claude, and click Approve once when the 4-digit codes match. Or copy the MCP config from Settings into Claude Desktop or another MCP app.

Your Google data

SafeContext reads your Gmail and, if you add it, your Google Calendar, with read-only permission you grant when you connect your Google account. It reads them so it can build a redacted, searchable version on your computer and let AI apps you choose search that version, such as Grok or Claude when you turn on MCP.

It cannot send, change, or delete your email or events. Your Google data is never sold, never used for ads, and never used to train AI models. The developer never stores a copy of it.

Use of Google user data adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
