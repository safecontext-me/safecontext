SafeContext for Mac

AI that knows your inbox.calendar.meetings.travel plans.inbox and calendar. Not your secrets.

SafeContext copies your Gmail, Google Calendar and iMessage conversations to your Mac, strips out codes, account numbers and other sensitive details right there, and lets the AI you choose, like Grok or Claude, search what's left.

Why SafeContext

  • Private by default

    Your copy lives only on your Mac, scrubbed before it's saved. Nothing leaves until you connect an AI.

  • Works with your AI

    Turn on MCP and add your private link to Grok or Claude, or add SafeContext to Claude Desktop or any MCP app on your Mac. Ask in plain words; answers point back to the source.

  • You stay in control

    Cut off AI access whenever you like. One click deletes your local copy and revokes SafeContext's access to Google.

Your Google data

SafeContext reads your Gmail and, if you add it, your Google Calendar, with read-only permission you grant when you connect your Google account. It reads them so it can build a scrubbed, searchable copy on your Mac and let AI apps you choose search that copy, such as Grok or Claude when you turn on MCP.

It cannot send, change or delete your email or events. Your Google data is never sold, never used for ads and never used to train AI models. The developer never stores a copy of it.
