SafeContext

A Mac app that gives AI assistants private context from your Gmail. Your email is scrubbed on your Mac before any AI can read it.

SafeContext asks for read-only access to your Gmail. Read the SafeContext Privacy Policy for exactly what it reads, where it is stored and when anything leaves your Mac.

What SafeContext does

SafeContext downloads your Gmail to your own Mac, removes sensitive information from it there, and saves a searchable copy on your computer. Turn on Use in Grok and you can ask Grok about your email, for example “when is my flight to Denver?” or “what did the landlord say about the lease?”, and get answers that cite the messages they came from.

Why it needs access to your Gmail

When you connect your Google account, SafeContext asks for one read-only Gmail permission:

  • Full mode (gmail.readonly): reads your messages and labels so it can scrub them and let you search them on your Mac.
  • Headers-only mode (gmail.metadata): reads only sender, recipients, subject and date, without message bodies.

SafeContext uses this access only to provide these features to you. It cannot send, delete or change your email.

How your data is protected

  • Stays on your Mac. Your scrubbed copy lives only on your computer. The developer never stores or keeps your email.
  • Scrubbed before it's saved. Verification codes, passwords, card and account numbers, API keys, addresses and similar details are removed. Security, banking, tax and health email is skipped entirely.
  • You choose what AI sees. Nothing is sent to an AI service until you turn on Use in Grok or connect an AI app yourself, and then only scrubbed results for its searches are shared.
  • Grok, without a cloud copy. Grok's searches reach your Mac through SafeContext's relay, which passes scrubbed results through without storing or logging them. It works only while your Mac is awake and SafeContext is open; switch it off, or regenerate the link, to cut Grok off.
  • No ads, no selling, no AI training. Your Google data is never used for advertising, never sold, and never used to train AI models.
  • Disconnect any time. One click in Settings deletes the local copy and revokes SafeContext's access to your Google account.

SafeContext is currently in a small private preview for macOS. Requires a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later).
