How it works

Scrubbed on your Mac. Searched by your AI.

Your mail and calendar are cleaned and stored on your own computer. An AI you connect only ever sees the scrubbed answers to its own searches.

  1. Connect Gmail and Calendar

    Sign in with Google and grant read-only access. Choose full messages or headers only. Add Google Calendar whenever you like.

  2. It's scrubbed on your Mac

    Each message and event is cleaned in memory, before anything is saved. Only the scrubbed copy is stored, on your Mac.

  3. Connect your AI

    Turn on Use in Grok, add your private link as a Grok connector and click Approve once. Or copy the MCP config from Settings into Claude Desktop or another MCP app.

  4. Just ask

    “When is my flight to Denver?” Your AI searches the scrubbed copy and answers with links back to the original email or event.

What gets removed

Scrubbing runs before anything is written to disk. Raw mail is never kept.

Skipped entirely

  • Security mail: sign-in and verification codes, password resets, new sign-in alerts
  • Banking, tax and health mail, by sender (banks, brokerages, tax services, insurers, pharmacies, clinics) and by subject (statements, tax forms, lab results, prescriptions)
  • Promotions, Social and Forums tabs, newsletters and other bulk mail
  • Spam, trash and drafts

Calendar events follow the same security, banking, tax and health rules. Dial-in PINs and meeting passcodes are never read.

Redacted in what's kept

  • One-time and backup codes
  • Passwords
  • API keys and tokens
  • Private keys
  • Card numbers and CVVs
  • Bank account and routing numbers
  • IBANs
  • Social Security numbers
  • Dates of birth
  • Phone numbers
  • Street addresses

Each one becomes a placeholder such as [REDACTED:CREDIT_CARD]. Quoted replies and signatures are trimmed, and attachments are never downloaded, only their file names.

What leaves your Mac, and when

  • Until you connect an AINothing. Your scrubbed copy stays on your Mac.
  • With Use in Grok onOnly the scrubbed results for each search Grok makes. Grok gets read-only search tools and can't change, send or delete anything.
  • Through the relayGrok reaches your Mac through relay.safecontext.me, which passes searches and results through in memory and doesn't store or log them. It keeps only a random link ID and one-way hashes of the secrets that recognise your Mac and Grok.
  • Grok, only while it's runningWhen your Mac is asleep or SafeContext is closed, Grok can't reach anything. An optional setting keeps your Mac awake while it's plugged in.
  • See what Grok askedThe app keeps an activity list on your Mac of what Grok asked for and how many results it got. Never the results themselves.
  • AI apps on your MacAn app you connect through MCP, like Claude Desktop, gets the same read-only search tools and reads your scrubbed copy directly on your Mac, with no relay. You choose whether to connect one.
  • After it reaches the AIResults Grok receives are handled by xAI under its own terms and privacy policy. An app you connect on your Mac handles results under its own terms, which may include sending them to its AI service.

How to turn it off

  • Pause GrokTurn the Use in Grok switch off. Nothing more is sent.
  • Disconnect a Mac appRemove SafeContext from that app's MCP settings.
  • Cut Grok off for goodClick Regenerate link. The old link stops working and every sign-in Grok held is forgotten. You can also remove the connector in Grok.
  • Delete everythingIn Settings, Disconnect and delete local mail removes your local copy and revokes SafeContext's access to your Google account. You can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
