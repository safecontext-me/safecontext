How it works

Scrubbed on your computer. Searched by your AI.

Your sources are cleaned and stored on your own computer. An AI you connect only ever sees the redacted answers to its own searches.

Giving an agent your whole inbox means it can read tax returns, password resets, and bank details, and act on what it reads. For the fuller argument, and five rules you can use even without SafeContext, see Working safely with AI agents.

  1. Connect your sources

    Sign in and grant read-only access. Connect email, calendar, and file storage.

    More coming soon iMessage

  2. Automatically scrubbed on your computer

    Each message and event is cleaned in memory, before anything is saved. Only the redacted, clean version is stored, on your computer. You get a Privacy Report showing what was kept, dropped, and redacted.

  3. Connect your AI

    Turn on MCP, add your private link as a connector in Grok or Claude, and click Approve once when the 4-digit codes match. Or copy the MCP config from Settings into Claude Desktop or another MCP app.

How the pieces connect

What gets removed

Scrubbing runs before anything is written to disk. Raw mail and messages are never kept.

Skipped entirely

  • Security mail: sign-in and verification codes, password resets, new sign-in alerts
  • Banking, tax, and health mail, by sender (banks, brokerages, tax services, insurers, pharmacies, clinics) and by subject (statements, tax forms, lab results, prescriptions)
  • Promotions, Social and Forums tabs, newsletters, and other bulk mail
  • Spam, trash, and drafts

Calendar events follow the same security, banking, tax, and health rules. Dial-in PINs, meeting passcodes, conference IDs and Webex access codes are removed before anything is saved.

Texts from short codes, sender IDs and toll-free numbers (banks, pharmacies, delivery and sign-in codes) are skipped, even if the number is saved in Contacts, and so is any text that reads like a bank, tax or health notice.

Redacted in what's kept

  • One-time and backup codes
  • Passwords
  • API keys and tokens
  • Private keys
  • Card numbers and CVVs
  • Bank account and routing numbers
  • IBANs
  • ID numbers
  • Social Security numbers
  • Dates of birth
  • Phone numbers
  • Street addresses

Each one becomes a placeholder such as [REDACTED:CREDIT_CARD]. Quoted replies and signatures are trimmed, and attachments are never downloaded, only their file names.

What leaves your computer, and when

  • Until you connect an AINothing. The redacted, cleaned version stays on your computer.
  • Update checksWhen it opens and every few hours, SafeContext asks safecontext.me whether there's a new version. The request carries none of your data, and you can turn the check off in Settings. Nothing installs until you choose to, and updates are verified as signed by SafeContext's developer first.
  • With MCP onOnly the redacted results for each search Grok or Claude makes. They get read-only search tools and cannot change, send, or delete anything. Searches pass through relay.safecontext.me, which does not store or log them. This only works while your computer is awake, SafeContext is open, and MCP is on.
  • You can inspect the workAfter sync, a Privacy Report on your computer shows what was kept, dropped, and redacted. The app also keeps an activity list of what Grok or Claude asked for and how many results it got, never the results themselves.
  • AI apps on your computerLike Claude Desktop, they get the same read-only search tools and read the redacted version directly, with no relay.
  • After it reaches the AIResults Grok receives are handled by xAI, and results Claude receives by Anthropic, each under its own terms and privacy policy.

Details of how the relay recognizes your computer, Grok, and Claude, including link identifiers and one-way hashes, belong in the Privacy Policy.

How to turn it off

  • Pause accessTurn the MCP switch off. Nothing more is sent.
  • Disconnect an appRemove SafeContext from that app's MCP settings.
  • Cut an app off for goodClick Regenerate link. The old link stops working and every sign-in Grok or Claude held is forgotten. You can also remove the connector in Grok or Claude.
  • Delete everythingIn Settings, Disconnect and delete local mail removes your local copy and revokes SafeContext's access to your Google account. You can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
