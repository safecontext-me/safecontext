SafeContext · your private context layer

Use your email and calendar in AI apps like Grok and Claude, with the sensitive parts removed first.

  • Stays on this Mac. Your mail is downloaded and stored here, never uploaded to SafeContext.
  • Scrubbed before it's saved. 2FA codes, passwords, card and account numbers, API keys, addresses and more are removed. Security, banking, tax and health email is skipped entirely.
  • You decide what AI sees. Turn on MCP and the AI apps you connect can search your scrubbed data, only while SafeContext is open. Nothing is sent until you do.
