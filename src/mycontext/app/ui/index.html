MyContext · your private context layer

Ask Grok about your email, with the sensitive parts removed first.

  • Stays on this Mac. Your mail is downloaded and stored here. There is no MyContext server; nothing is uploaded except what you ask an AI about.
  • Scrubbed before it's saved. 2FA codes, passwords, card and account numbers, API keys, addresses and more are removed. Security, banking, tax and health email is skipped entirely.
  • You decide what Grok sees. Only the scrubbed messages Grok searches for are sent to xAI, and only when you ask a question.
